Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year. Leave must be approved by a line manager at least 2 weeks in advance. Unused leave can be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

## Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote work must be agreed with the line manager. Employees working remotely are still expected to attend all scheduled meetings.

## Equipment Policy
Acme Corp provides a laptop and monitor to all full-time employees. Contractors must supply their own equipment. Equipment must be returned on the last day of employment.

## Expense Policy
Business expenses up to $50 can be reimbursed without prior approval. Expenses above $50 require manager approval before being incurred. All expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred with receipts.

## Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are held twice a year: in June and December. Employees receive a rating of 1 to 5. A rating of 3 or above is considered meeting expectations. Employees with a rating of 2 or below for two consecutive reviews may be placed on a performance improvement plan.

## Salary and Bonuses
Salaries are reviewed annually in January. Bonuses are paid in February based on the December performance review rating. Employees with a rating of 4 receive a 5% bonus. Employees with a rating of 5 receive a 10% bonus.

## Termination Policy
Employees must give 4 weeks notice before resigning. The company may terminate employment with 4 weeks notice except in cases of gross misconduct, where termination may be immediate. Final pay including unused leave is processed within 7 days of the last working day.
